# Project: EEG Grant — Proposal & Funding Strategy

*This document outlines the conceptual framework, specific aims, and funding roadmap for a grant proposal based on the pilot electrophysiological dataset of cognitive stabilization via dietary creatine supplementation. This strategy is tailored to academic and clinical review panels, with a specific focus on the Indian scientific funding ecosystem.*

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & "THE PITCH"

### The Problem: The "Behavioral Ceiling" in Nutritional Trials
Traditional clinical and nutritional trials evaluating dietary supplements (such as creatine) in healthy populations frequently yield null findings. Because healthy young adults perform at near-ceiling levels on basic cognitive tasks under normal conditions, coarse behavioral metrics—such as Reaction Time (RT) and accuracy averages—are often not sensitive enough to capture treatment-induced changes.

### The Innovation: ERP Variance as a Bioenergetic Proxy
This proposal introduces a novel methodology: utilizing **event-related potential (ERP) variability** as a sensitive, millisecond-level biomarker of neural efficiency and brain bioenergetics. 

Our preliminary pilot data indicates that while average cognitive performance remains similar between groups, **long-term creatine supplementation significantly stabilizes neural recruitment (reducing N200 and P300 latency/amplitude variance)** during low-demand task states (Stroop Congruent trials). Under high-demand task states (Stroop Incongruent trials), top-down executive networks impose strong network-level constraints that compress this variance, demonstrating a condition-dependent **Bioenergetic-Task Constraint Model**.

## 2. SPECIFIC AIMS

### Aim 1: Validate the effects of chronic creatine supplementation on N200/P300 ERP variability in a fully powered cohort.
* **Objective**: Scale the pilot study to a double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial (RCT) with a fully powered cohort ($N=60$; 30 per group).
* **Rationale**: Verify if 6 months of daily oral creatine monohydrate supplementation (5 g/day) consistently reduces inter-individual and trial-by-trial intra-individual variance in P300 peak latency (Pz electrode) and N200 mean amplitude (FC1/FC2 electrodes) during low-demand cognitive states.
* **Hypothesis**: Creatine supplementation will selectively reduce electrophysiological variance during low-demand cognitive conditions (0-back / Stroop congruent) without altering average downstream behavioral metrics, confirming that ERP variance is a more sensitive marker of neural metabolic status than behavioral averages.

### Aim 2: Validate ERP variance as a bioenergetic biomarker using multi-modal $^{31}\text{P}$-Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy ($^{31}\text{P}$-MRS).
* **Objective**: Directly correlate scalp-recorded electrophysiological stability with in vivo brain high-energy phosphate kinetics.
* **Rationale**: EEG measures postsynaptic potentials at the scalp, which is an indirect downstream marker of metabolic status. Pairing EEG with $^{31}\text{P}$-MRS will validate whether the reduction in ERP variance is physically driven by elevated cerebral energy reserves.
* **Hypothesis**: The reduction in P300 latency and N200 amplitude variance in the supplemented group will directly and positively correlate with elevated concentrations of brain phosphocreatine (PCr) and ATP, as well as an optimized PCr/Pi ratio in the prefrontal and parietal cortices.

### Aim 3: Assess whether stabilized baseline neural recruitment translates to cognitive resilience under metabolic stress.
* **Objective**: Evaluate the functional, real-world utility of creatine-induced neural stabilization.
* **Rationale**: If creatine stabilizes baseline neural recruitment, it should protect cognitive function when the brain is subjected to acute metabolic challenges.
* **Hypothesis**: Supplemented individuals exhibiting stabilized baseline ERP profiles will show significantly greater resistance to cognitive fatigue (maintained RT and accuracy consistency) during a prolonged, continuous performance task under conditions of mild sleep deprivation (24-hour sub-total deprivation) compared to the control group.

## 3. TARGET FUNDING SCHEMES (INDIAN CONTEXT)

### A. Science & Technology Focus (DST & SERB)
1. **DST-CSRI (Cognitive Science Research Initiative)**
   * **Scope**: Highly specialized initiative run by the Department of Science and Technology.
   * **Fit**: **Excellent (Primary Target)**. The scheme specifically funds projects exploring cognitive paradigms (Stroop, N-back), neuroimaging (EEG/ERP), and behavioral interventions to understand human cognition.
   * **Strategy**: Pitch the project as a novel method to index cognitive resource allocation and neural efficiency using electrophysiological variance.
2. **SERB-CRG (Core Research Grant)**
   * **Scope**: Flagship extramural research grant for individual PIs in India.
   * **Fit**: **High**. Reviewed under the **Life Sciences (Physiology and Neuroscience)** committee.
   * **Strategy**: Request funding for upgrading laboratory equipment (e.g., higher-density active EEG caps, eye-trackers) and supporting personnel (Project Associate/Junior Research Fellow).

### B. Biomedical & Public Health Focus (ICMR & DBT)
1. **ICMR Ad-hoc Research Schemes**
   * **Scope**: Funding for biomedical, clinical, and translational health research.
   * **Fit**: **Good**. Focuses on the preventative utility of nutraceuticals.
   * **Strategy**: Frame the project from a public health and preventative geriatrics perspective. Pitch creatine as a low-cost, widely accessible dietary supplement to preserve cognitive reserve and prevent age-related cognitive decline in the aging Indian demographic.
2. **DBT R&D Project Grants**
   * **Scope**: Funding for biotechnology, biochemistry, and life science research.
   * **Fit**: **Good**. Apply under the **"Nutrition and Wellness"** or **"Human Development and Disease"** panels.
   * **Strategy**: Focus on the bioenergetic mechanisms of the muscle-brain axis, highlighting creatine's role in brain ATP homeostasis and synaptic plasticity.

### C. Fellowship & Career Development (DST & DBT India Alliance)
1. **DBT/Wellcome Trust India Alliance (Early/Intermediate Fellowship)**
   * **Scope**: Prestigious biomedical fellowships for early-career researchers and postdocs.
   * **Fit**: **High**. Suitable if your student is aiming to lead this project for their postdoctoral work.
2. **DST-CSRI Postdoctoral Fellowship**
   * **Scope**: Dedicated fellowship for young researchers in cognitive science.

## 4. PROPOSAL CHECKLIST & CRITICAL CONTROLS

Reviewers will critique the proposal on specific methodological parameters. Ensure these controls are addressed in the methodology:

- [ ] **Dietary Tracking**: Screen and record baseline dietary creatine intake. Stratify or match groups based on omnivore vs. vegetarian/vegan diets, as vegetarians exhibit greater brain sensitivity to supplementation due to lower baseline tissue stores.
- [ ] **Multi-Centric Collaboration**: For Aim 2 ($^{31}\text{P}$-MRS), establish a formal collaboration with a premier national neuroimaging facility (e.g., NIMHANS in Bangalore, NBRC in Manesar, or AIIMS in New Delhi) to secure dedicated scan time.
- [ ] **Gender-Stratified Analysis**: Ensure equal representation of male and female participants. Females exhibit distinct hormonal fluctuations during menses, pregnancy, and menopause that affect creatine kinase kinetics; the proposal should actively control for menstrual cycle phase during testing sessions.
- [ ] **Manpower & JRF Support**: Include salaries for at least one JRF (Junior Research Fellow) to handle participant recruitment, screening, dietary logging, and data processing.
